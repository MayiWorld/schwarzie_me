🌌 Meet the Schwarzie Radius

Cool Fact: If Earth were compressed into a black hole, it would be only about 9 millimetres across—roughly the size of a marble.

The Schwarzschild radius—affectionately known here as the “Schwarzie”—marks the boundary where gravity becomes so powerful that not even light can escape. Cross this invisible threshold, and you enter the mysterious realm of a black hole.

This interactive experiment lets you calculate your own hypothetical Schwarzie. Enter a mass, add a touch of spin, and compare yourself with Earth, the Sun, and the giants of the cosmos.
